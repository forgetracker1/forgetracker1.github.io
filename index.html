Log faster

Questions

Is it really free? What’s the catch?+

There isn’t one. Every feature is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscription. It’s built by an independent developer who wanted a great free tracker to exist.

Do I need an account?+

No. Open the app and start lifting. Everything is saved on your iPhone.

Does it work without signal in the gym?+

Yes, completely offline. The only thing that needs internet is the optional video-tutorial link.

What do I need?+

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later. The Apple Watch app needs watchOS 10 or later and is optional.

Can I bring my data with me if I leave?+

Yes. Profile → Export Workouts gives you every set as a CSV file that opens in any spreadsheet.

Kilograms or pounds?+

Both. Switch any time in Profile, and your history converts automatically.
