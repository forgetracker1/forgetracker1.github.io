Privacy Policy

Forge for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated November 4, 2026

Short version: Forge does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you log stays on your own devices.

What Forge stores

Your workouts, routines, custom exercises, notes, body-weight entries and settings are saved only on your iPhone, using Apple’s on-device storage. If you use the Apple Watch app, your current workout is sent directly between your iPhone and your Apple Watch over Apple’s WatchConnectivity link. Nothing passes through our servers. Forge doesn’t have any servers.

What Forge does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No collection of your name, email, location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Forge schedules a local notification on your device when a rest timer ends. These notifications are created and delivered on your device; no data is sent anywhere.

Links to other sites

The “Watch Video Tutorials” button opens a YouTube search in your browser or the YouTube app. From that point YouTube’s own privacy policy applies. Forge does not send YouTube any of your workout data, only the name of the exercise you are searching for.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export all your workouts as a CSV file from Profile → Export Workouts. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple may include Forge’s on-device data in that backup, under Apple’s own privacy terms.

Children

Forge does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
